Good questions before gutter work.

Can I request cleaning without committing to new gutters?

Yes. Start by discussing the clearing work you want assessed. A cleaning scope can identify included channels and outlets, while any visible repair concern is recorded separately. Finding an issue during cleaning should lead to an explanation and a decision, rather than automatically expanding the work.

What if only one side of the house has a problem?

Point out that side and describe the timing of the symptom. A roof valley, blocked outlet, loose connection or another condition may be involved. The estimate can focus on the affected runs while explaining whether their connections to the rest of the system need review.

How should I compare a 5-inch and a 6-inch proposal?

Compare the complete arrangements, not only the channel widths. Ask which roof sections each proposal covers, how many outlets are included, what downspouts are specified and where water will discharge. Material, attachment details and the work required at existing gutters should also be clear.

Should I check association requirements before choosing a finish?

If your property is subject to an association or exterior-review process, establish the requirements before selecting a visible color, profile or downspout route. We do not assume that one community’s rules apply to another. Bring any approval information you already have into the planning conversation.
